Paul Volcker

Chair of the Federal Reserve, 1979–1987

Paul Volcker was Chair of the Federal Reserve from 1979 to 1987, appointed by President Carter and reappointed by President Reagan. He is best known for raising interest rates sharply to bring down the high inflation of the late 1970s. He earlier led the Federal Reserve Bank of New York and later advised President Obama on the economy. He died in 2019. The documents are statements, speeches and testimony from his time as Chair, kept in the Federal Reserve's FRASER archive. MouthMath counts 20 documents from 1986 to 1987.
