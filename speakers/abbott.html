Greg Abbott

Governor of Texas (R-TX)

Greg Abbott has been Governor of Texas since 2015. A Republican, he previously served as Texas Attorney General and as a justice on the Texas Supreme Court. As governor he signs or vetoes bills passed by the Legislature and leads the state's emergency response. Governors lead their state's executive branch, sign or veto bills and manage state agencies.
