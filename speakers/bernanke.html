Ben Bernanke

Chair of the Federal Reserve, 2006–2014

Ben Bernanke was Chair of the Federal Reserve from 2006 to 2014. A former Princeton University economics professor who studied the Great Depression, he led the Fed through the 2008 financial crisis and the slow recovery that followed. In 2022 he shared the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for research on banks and financial crises. The documents are speeches and testimony to Congress given as Chair, from the Federal Reserve's official website. MouthMath counts 20 documents from 2013 to 2014.
