Christopher Waller

Governor of the Federal Reserve

Christopher Waller has served on the Federal Reserve Board of Governors since December 2020. An economist, he earlier served as executive vice president and director of research at the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis and taught at the University of Notre Dame and other universities. His research focuses on monetary policy and how central banks work. Fed governors are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate for terms of up to 14 years.
