Michelle Bowman

Vice Chair for Supervision of the Federal Reserve

Michelle Bowman has served on the Federal Reserve Board of Governors since November 2018 and became Vice Chair for Supervision in June 2025, leading the Fed's oversight of banks. She earlier served as Kansas State Bank Commissioner and was a vice president of a community bank in Kansas. She was the first governor appointed to fill the Board seat reserved for someone with community banking experience. Fed governors are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate for terms of up to 14 years.
