Apple CEO, Aug 2011–Aug 2026 (handed over to John Ternus; now Executive Chairman)

Tim Cook was CEO of Apple from August 2011 until August 2026, when he handed the role to John Ternus and became Executive Chairman. He joined Apple in 1998 to run its operations and supply chain. As CEO he oversaw new products such as the Apple Watch and AirPods and the growth of Apple's services.
