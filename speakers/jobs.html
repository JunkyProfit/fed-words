Steve Jobs

Apple CEO, Sept 1997–Aug 2011 (interim CEO until 2000); Apple co-founder

Steve Jobs co-founded Apple in 1976. After leaving in 1985, he founded NeXT and led Pixar, then returned to Apple in 1997 and served as CEO until August 2011. His later years at Apple brought the iMac, iPod, iPhone and iPad. He died in October 2011. The documents are his own public words filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission.
