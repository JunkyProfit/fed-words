Ronald Reagan

40th President of the United States

Ronald Reagan was the 40th President of the United States, serving two terms from 1981 to 1989. A Republican, he earlier worked as a film actor and served two terms as Governor of California. He died in 2004. The speeches come from The American Presidency Project at UC Santa Barbara and include inaugural addresses, State of the Union messages and addresses to the nation.
