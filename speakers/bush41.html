George H. W. Bush

41st President of the United States

George H. W. Bush was the 41st President of the United States, serving from 1989 to 1993. A Republican and a World War II Navy pilot, he earlier served as Vice President under Ronald Reagan, as CIA Director and as U.S. ambassador to the United Nations. He died in 2018. The speeches come from The American Presidency Project at UC Santa Barbara and include inaugural addresses, State of the Union messages and addresses to the nation.
