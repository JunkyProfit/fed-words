Spencer J. Cox

Governor of Utah (R-UT)

Spencer Cox has been Governor of Utah since 2021. A Republican and a lawyer from the small town of Fairview, he previously served as Utah's lieutenant governor, in the Utah House of Representatives and as a county commissioner. Governors lead their state's executive branch, sign or veto bills and manage state agencies.
