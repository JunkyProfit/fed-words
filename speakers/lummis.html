Cynthia M. Lummis

U.S. Senator (R-WY)

Cynthia Lummis has represented Wyoming in the U.S. Senate since 2021. A Republican and a rancher, she earlier served as Wyoming State Treasurer and represented the state in the U.S. House. She grew up on her family's ranch near Cheyenne, Wyoming, and later earned a law degree. Senators serve six-year terms, and each state elects two.
