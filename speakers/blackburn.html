Marsha Blackburn

U.S. Senator (R-TN)

Marsha Blackburn has represented Tennessee in the U.S. Senate since 2019, the first woman elected to the Senate from the state. A Republican, she earlier served eight terms in the U.S. House. Before Congress, she ran a marketing business and served in the Tennessee State Senate. Senators serve six-year terms, and each state elects two.
