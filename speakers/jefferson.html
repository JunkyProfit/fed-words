Philip Jefferson

Vice Chair of the Federal Reserve

Philip Jefferson has served on the Federal Reserve Board of Governors since May 2022 and became Vice Chair in September 2023. An economist who studies poverty and the labor market, he earlier taught at Swarthmore College and served as vice president for academic affairs and dean of faculty at Davidson College. He also worked as a research economist at the Federal Reserve Board early in his career. Fed governors are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate for terms of up to 14 years.
