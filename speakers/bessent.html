Scott Bessent

79th U.S. Secretary of the Treasury

Scott Bessent became the 79th Secretary of the Treasury in January 2025. Before government service he spent decades as an investor, including at Soros Fund Management, and founded the investment firm Key Square Group. The Treasury manages the federal government's finances, borrowing and tax collection. Cabinet secretaries are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate.
