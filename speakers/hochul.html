Kathy Hochul

Governor of New York (D-NY)

Kathy Hochul became Governor of New York in 2021, the first woman to hold the office. A Democrat, she earlier served as lieutenant governor and represented a western New York district in the U.S. House. Governors lead their state's executive branch, sign or veto bills and manage state agencies.
