Jerome Powell

Chair of the Federal Reserve, 2018–2026

Jerome Powell served as Chair of the Federal Reserve from 2018 to 2026, after joining the Fed's Board of Governors in 2012. A lawyer by training, he worked in investment banking and private equity and served at the Treasury Department under President George H. W. Bush. As Chair he led the Fed through the pandemic and the rise in inflation that followed. The documents are speeches and testimony to Congress given as Chair, from the Federal Reserve's official website.
