Bob Ferguson

Governor of Washington (D-WA)

Bob Ferguson became Governor of Washington in January 2025. A Democrat, he previously served three terms as the state's Attorney General and earlier sat on the King County Council. He grew up in Seattle. Governors lead their state's executive branch, sign or veto bills and manage state agencies.
