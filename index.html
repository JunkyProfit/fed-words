    From Mouth to Math

    Word counts from official speeches, testimony, letters and court opinions by Fed Chairmen, CEOs and US Government officials.

    MouthMath was created to offer a quantitative view of public speech. By measuring how often each word is used, it lets anyone grasp the substance of a speech at a glance.

    This site was created with transparency and truth in mind. It is politically neutral, with no bias: every speaker is counted the same way.

    MouthMath counts and ranks every word in Federal Reserve Chair speeches and testimony, CEO earnings calls and shareholder letters from major banks and companies, and remarks by US government officials, so you can see at a glance which words, like inflation, interest rates or AI, come up most.

    All content comes only from publicly available sources and is gathered on a best-effort basis. Counts are updated as new material becomes available.
