    About Mouth Math

    This site was built by someone who believes in transparency and truth. It counts every word in official, publicly available texts by public figures, taken from each official source:

    • Fed Chair: speech and testimony transcripts published on federalreserve.gov.
    • CEOs: shareholder letters posted by each company on its own website (palantir.com, aboutamazon.com, berkshirehathaway.com).
    • U.S. President: official transcripts from the Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents (U.S. Government Publishing Office, govinfo.gov), the official record of the President's remarks.
    • Pope: English texts published by the Holy See on vatican.va (© Dicastery for Communication – Libreria Editrice Vaticana).

    Licensing approach. Works of the U.S. government (Fed and White House transcripts) are in the public domain, so every sentence is available on hover. Other texts (CEO letters, Vatican texts) are copyrighted by their publishers: for those we publish only word counts and short excerpts (at most about a quarter of each text, and at most 10 sentences per word), each linked to the original, and never the full text.

    Translations. Where a text was originally given in another language, we use the official English translation published by that government or institution. Counts reflect that published English text.

    Neutrality. We present word counts without commentary. Nothing is edited or interpreted; footnotes, editorial notes and other speakers' words are left out, and every sentence shown links back to its source so you can check it yourself. Inclusion of a person does not imply endorsement, and this site is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government, institution, company, or person listed.

    How it works: words are lowercased and counted across the documents you select. “Hide common stopwords” removes very common words like “the” and “and”. Press conference Q&A isn't included yet.
