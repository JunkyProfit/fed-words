Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

In short: Mouth Math (mouthmath.com) has no accounts, no login, no sign-up and no forms. The site itself does not collect, store or sell personal data and does not set its own cookies. Ads on the site are served by Google AdSense, and Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to show those ads, including personalized ads.

Information the site collects

None that identifies you. Mouth Math is a static website: the word counts are computed in your browser, and your choices (person, documents, view) are kept only in the page address so you can share a link. We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, or our own cookies or local storage, and we don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

Mouth Math uses Google AdSense, an advertising service from Google, to show ads. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google and its partners may place and read cookies in your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses and similar identifiers, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Your browser automatically sends Google information such as the address of the page you're viewing and your IP address.
  • Learn more in Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Personalized ads and your choices. You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com). If you opt out you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests or browsing history. You can also opt out of some other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info (US) or www.youronlinechoices.eu (Europe), and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

If ads from other ad networks or vendors are shown through AdSense, those vendors may also use cookies as described above; you can opt out of their personalized advertising through the links above. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), cookies for personalized ads are only used with your consent, and you can change your choice at any time.

TradingView stock charts

On CEO pages, clicking a stock ticker opens a price chart embedded from TradingView. The chart is loaded only when you click, directly from TradingView's servers, so TradingView receives your IP address and browser information and may set its own cookies. That is governed by the TradingView Privacy Policy.

Other services

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Animated word cloud: when Motion is on, the p5.js library is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which receives standard request information such as your IP address; see the jsDelivr privacy policy.
  • Links to sources: links to official documents open the publisher's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Mouth Math is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
